# Wiki Configuration

## Scope

Public tools the owner heard of and judged useful in work: plugins and skill collections for coding agents, libraries, apps and command-line tools, models, runtimes and patterns. Only what was mentioned to the owner or read about and found useful; not a survey of everything popular. The employer's own open-source repositories and internal or marketplace tools stay out. Design: `docs/catalog-design.md`; card quality bar: `docs/catalog-card-standard.md`.

## Conventions

- This topic is public (the kit is MIT). Trials and measurements are retold by kind of task, with no project, person, customer, employer or ticket names, no internal hosts and no private paths.
- Clones are written as `$EXTERNAL/<repo>` (the repository name without its owner); every user defines `$EXTERNAL` in their own global rules.
- The catalog installs and updates nothing. A card never gives an install step that acts without the user's yes; plugins run only in a one-session sandbox (`claude --plugin-dir`), packages only in a throwaway environment.
- The card is the only master of a part's status. `wiki/topics/task-index.md`, the area pages and the science section pages are generated by `scripts/catalog_index.py` and never edited by hand.
- Every number, date, version and quoted phrase in an article appears word for word in a raw file it cites: `scripts/check_evidence.py --strict catalog`.
- Raw files are immutable: a new desk check of a new version is a new file, and the card's `sources:` moves to it.
