# Triggers, phrasing and aids

Kept in the language they were written in, tagged `lang`; add your language as
a new row or block, never replace an original.

## Triggers

| lang | phrase | mode |
|---|---|---|
| en | tell a bedtime story, a story for my kid, a fairy tale about | story |
| en | continue the story, next episode | continue |
| ru | расскажи сказку, сказка на ночь, сказку про | story |
| ru | продолжи сказку, что было дальше | continue |

## Length by age

| age | reading time | words |
|---|---|---|
| 3-4 | 2-3 min | 250-400 |
| 5-6 | 4-5 min | 500-750 |
| 7-9 | 6-8 min | 800-1200 |

```markdown
|---|---|---|---|
```

## Sleepy endings

- `lang: en` "The wind hummed softly, the stars blinked one by one, and the
  little squirrel curled up, warm and safe, and drifted off to sleep."
- `lang: ru` «Ветер тихонько напевал, звёзды зажигались одна за другой, а
  бельчонок свернулся клубочком — тепло, спокойно — и уснул.»
