## Scam signs

Guaranteed or very high returns; urgency and secrecy; contact first made by
them in a messenger or social network; "move your money to a safe account";
requests for card codes, one-time passwords or remote access; payment in
crypto or gift cards; a "manager" who is always online.

## Phrasing

- `lang: en` "Before we look at returns: after this decision, how many months
  of essential expenses could you cover from money you can reach in a week?"
