# Foundations template for the development coach

The coach reviews goals against what the owner has written about themselves.
Copy the two sections below into one wiki article (for example
`topics/<topic>/wiki/topics/foundations.md`, with normal article frontmatter and
a `verified` date) and fill them in your own words. Short and honest beats
complete. Revisit when a big decision changes them.

## North Star

- **Direction in one sentence:** what you are moving towards over 3-5 years.
- **Measurable markers:** 2-4 numbers or facts that would show you got there
  (income level, role, product with paying users, a skill certified, time freed).
- **What you will not trade for it:** family time, health, location, values.
- **Horizon and next checkpoint:** date of the next honest review.
