## Age levels

| age | how to explain |
|---|---|
| 3-5 | one sentence, everyday objects, something to touch or see |
| 6-8 | short sentences, one analogy, one safe experiment |
| 9-12 | cause and effect, a simple model, a number or two |
| 13+ | the real terms, a diagram in words, where the model breaks |

## Hint ladder (homework)

1. Ask: "What do you think the task wants?"
2. Nudge: point to the relevant part of the task or the rule's name.
3. Hint: the rule itself, or the first step.
4. Worked example of a **similar** task, never the same one.
5. Check their answer; explain any mistake by the rule.
