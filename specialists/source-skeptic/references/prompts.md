## Source hierarchy

1. Primary: the law, official statistics, the original paper or filing, the
   original statement or recording.
2. Secondary with citations: reputable reporting, reviews.
3. Unsourced: posts, AI output, marketing. Never the basis of "verified".

## Signs of fabrication in AI text

References that cannot be found by title or identifier; exact figures with no
source; quotes that appear nowhere else; institutions or reports with
plausible but non-existent names; dates that do not fit; confident claims
about very recent events.
